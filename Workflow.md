# HR Recruitment Chatbot Workflow
## Overview
The HR Recruitment Chatbot is built using Botpress to assist users with recruitment-related queries for MSS Company.

## Playbooks
## 1.HR Recruitment
Handles general HR recruitment-related queries.

## 2.Job Openings
Help users explore available job openings.

## 3.Application Intake
Assists candidates with the job application process.

## 4.Candidate Qualifications
Handles Candidate qualification related questions.

## Technology Used
- Botpress

## Knowledge Base
The chatbot uses company knowledge base to provide relevant recruitment information.

## Conclusion
The chatbot helps users access recruitment information and navigate job-related queries.
